# Info
Trepsin was a 4 armed troll who is fiercely loyal to [[Characters/The Hunting Lodge/Talis]] even after her death. Trepsin served as the hunter and groundskeeper of [[Atlas/Sword Coast/Greypeak Mountains/The Hunting Lodge]] while it was occupied by the [[Factions/Cult of the Dragon]]. Trepsin was assisted by his slightly less intelligent and only 2 armed helpers [[Characters/The Hunting Lodge/Dirk en Dirk]].
