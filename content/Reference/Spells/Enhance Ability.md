# Enhance Ability

*2nd-level transmutation*

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (fur or a feather from a beast)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour.

You touch a creature and bestow upon it a magical enhancement. Choose one of the following effects; the target gains that effect until the spell ends.

> [!info]- 🐻 ***Bear's Endurance***
> - [[Advantage]] on [[Constitution checks]]. 
> - 2D6 temporary HP, which are lost when the spell ends.

> [!info]- 🐂 ***Bull's Strength***
> - [[Advantage]] on [[Strength checks]]
> - Carrying capacity doubles.

> [!info]- 🐈 ***Cat's Grace***
> - [[Advantage]] on [[Dexterity checks]]
> - No damage from falling 20 ft or less if it isn't incapacitated.

> [!info]- 🦅 ***Eagle's Splendor***
> - [[Advantage]] on [[Charisma checks]].

> [!info]- 🦊 ***Fox's Cunning***
> - [[Advantage]] on [[Intelligence checks]].

> [!info]- 🦉 ***Owl's Wisdom***
> - [[Advantage]] on [[Wisdom checks]].

***At Higher Levels***. 
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.
